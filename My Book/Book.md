# The Blackwood Ledger

## Premise
Former detective Mara Winston is hired by the charming, evasive Arthur Vane to recover a ledger stolen from his family's manor. The ledger records every debt owed to the Arcane Syndicate — and Mara's name is in it.

## Genre and tone
Gaslamp mystery with a thread of quiet magic. Rain, lamp oil, old money. Tense and wry rather than grim; violence is sudden and brief.

## Style guide
- Close third person, past tense, one POV per chapter.
- Short paragraphs. Let dialogue carry the scene.
- No modern idioms. No swearing.
- Magic is never explained, only noticed.

## Voice sample
The rain had been at it since noon, patient as a debt collector. Mara stood under the manor's dripping eaves and counted the lit windows. Seven. A house that burned that much oil was either very rich or very frightened. In her experience it was usually both.
