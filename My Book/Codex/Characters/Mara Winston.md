# Mara Winston

## Description
Former detective of the city constabulary, dismissed after she refused to bury a Syndicate case. Now takes private work for people who can't go to the police. Dry humor, doesn't trust easily, notices everything.

## Voice
Clipped sentences. Never swears. Calls Arthur "Vane". Deflects with understatement.
