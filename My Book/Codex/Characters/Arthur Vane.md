# Arthur Vane

## Description
Heir to Blackwood Manor and to its debts. Charming, well-dressed, and always a half-step from the truth. Hires Mara to recover the family ledger.

## Voice
Warm and courtly. Answers questions with questions. Says "my dear detective" when he is nervous.
