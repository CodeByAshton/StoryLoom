# Elias Crane

## Description
Mara's old partner from the constabulary. Left the force the same winter she did and now keeps the books at the harbormaster's office. Patient, tired, loyal to a fault.

## Voice
Slow and plain. Uses sailors' sayings he doesn't quite believe.
